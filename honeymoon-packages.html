Your Honeymoon Questions

When is the best time for a Sri Lanka honeymoon?
It depends on the coast. The south and west coasts (Galle, Mirissa) are best from November to April with dry, sunny weather. The east coast (Trincomalee, Passikudah) shines from May to September. We'll guide you to the perfect location for your dates.
How far in advance should we book?
For the best selection of exclusive villas and boutique hotels, we recommend planning 6 to 9 months in advance, especially for peak season travel (December-March). Last-minute arrangements are sometimes possible, but advance booking ensures your ideal accommodations.
Can you handle special surprises or celebrations?
Absolutely. We specialize in creating magical moments—whether it's arranging a marriage proposal setup, a special anniversary celebration during your honeymoon, or accommodating any special requests to make your trip uniquely yours.
Are all activities and tours private?
Yes. Every guided experience, transfer, and activity we include is for you and your partner only. Your time together is sacred, and we ensure it remains undisturbed and completely private.
