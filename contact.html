Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about planning your luxury Sri Lanka journey

How soon will you respond to my inquiry?

We guarantee a response within 24 hours during business days. For urgent inquiries, please call our 24/7 number for immediate assistance.

How far in advance should I book?

For luxury accommodations and peak season (December-April), we recommend booking 6-9 months in advance. For last-minute trips, contact us for availability.

Will I have a dedicated travel consultant?

Yes, you'll be assigned a personal travel consultant who will handle all aspects of your journey, from initial planning to post-trip follow-up.

What safety measures do you have?

We partner with verified accommodations and transportation providers, offer 24/7 in-country support, and provide comprehensive travel insurance options.
